Rocket drawing completed on Excalidraw whiteboard canvas.

#### Elements Drawn:
- **Nose Cone:** Symmetrical pointed triangular nose cone positioned atop the rocket fuselage.
- **Fuselage:** Tall cylindrical rocket body with curved corners.
- **Stripes:** Dual decorative horizontal bands across the upper fuselage.
- **Porthole Window:** Concentric circular window structure featuring a dual-ring rim and blue glass fill.
- **Fins:** Left and right aerodynamic wing fins flanking the lower fuselage.
- **Engine Base & Nozzles:** Dedicated engine transition mount supporting twin exhaust nozzles.
- **Flames:** Multi-tiered rocket thrust exhaust plumes descending beneath the engine nozzles.
- **Background Stars:** Constellation of diamond stars framing the upper trajectory of the rocket.

#### Limitations & Deviations:
- Whiteboard canvas uses freeform sketch styling rather than smooth Bezier curve vector meshes.
- Line segments forming multi-part contours (such as the outer flame outlines and fin edges) rely on discrete path strokes rather than closed vector polygons with automatic flood fills.
