#### Top Research Papers on Multi-Agent Orchestration

- **Multi-Agent Collaboration Mechanisms: A Survey of LLMs**
  - **Authors:** K. T. Tran, D. Dao, M. D. Nguyen, Q. V. Pham, et al.
  - **Paper Link:** [arXiv:2501.06322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06322)
  - **Key Focus:** Comprehensive taxonomy and survey of collaboration, communication topologies, and orchestration paradigms (centralized, decentralized, hierarchical, and hybrid) across LLM multi-agent systems.

- **Multi-Agent Collaboration via Evolving Orchestration**
  - **Authors:** Y. Dang, C. Qian, X. Luo, J. Fan, Z. Xie, et al.
  - **Publication:** NeurIPS
  - **Paper Link:** [NeurIPS Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f1320d2e2842169c6fc89dcbd80e94d0-Abstract-Conference.html)
  - **Key Focus:** Dynamic workflow adaptation where orchestration structures evolve during task execution rather than relying on static prompt chains or rigid state machines.

- **Architectural Design, Not Only Model Intelligence, Governs Multi-Agent LLM Performance**
  - **Authors:** A. Orogat, A. Rostam, E. Mansour
  - **Paper Link:** [arXiv:2602.03128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03128)
  - **Key Focus:** Empirical benchmarking analyzing how orchestration topology, routing policies, and shared memory abstractions impact task completion rates independently of underlying base LLM capacity.

- **HALO: Hierarchical Autonomous Logic-Oriented Orchestration for Multi-Agent LLM Systems**
  - **Authors:** Z. Hou, J. Tang, Y. Wang
  - **Paper Link:** [arXiv:2505.13516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13516)
  - **Key Focus:** Hierarchical planning framework dividing global task decomposition from localized sub-agent tool execution to mitigate communication drift and context degradation.

- **OSC: Cognitive Orchestration through Dynamic Knowledge Alignment in Multi-Agent LLM Collaboration**
  - **Authors:** J. Zhang, Y. Fan, K. Cai, J. Tang, X. Sun, K. Wang
  - **Publication:** EMNLP Findings
  - **Paper Link:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/anthology-files/pdf/findings/2025.findings-emnlp.335.pdf)
  - **Key Focus:** Dynamic synchronization protocols designed to prevent hallucination propagation and align latent knowledge representations between orchestrator and worker agents.

- **Beyond the Strongest LLM: Multi-Turn Multi-Agent Orchestration vs. Single LLMs on Benchmarks**
  - **Authors:** A. X. Tian, R. Zhang, J. Tang, Y. M. Cho, X. Li, Q. Yi, et al.
  - **Paper Link:** [arXiv:2509.23537](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23537)
  - **Key Focus:** Evaluation of orchestrated multi-turn agent networks against frontier monolithic models, quantifying overhead, consensus mechanisms, and reasoning reliability.
