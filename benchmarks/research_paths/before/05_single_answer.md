#### Foundational Multi-Agent Orchestration Framework Papers

- **AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation**
  - **Authors:** Qingyun Wu, Gagan Bansal, Jieyu Zhang, Yiran Wu, Shaokun Zhang, Erkang Zhu, Chi Wang, et al. (Microsoft Research)
  - **Link:** [arXiv:2308.08155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08155)
  - **Focus:** Introduces conversable multi-agent abstractions. Orchestration is structured via turn-based and event-driven conversation flows across customizable agents and human-in-the-loop nodes.

- **MetaGPT: Meta Programming for A Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework**
  - **Authors:** Sirui Hong, Mingchen Zhuge, Jonathan Chen, Xiawu Zheng, Cheng Hao, Ceyao Zhang, Jinlin Wang, et al. (DeepWisdom)
  - **Link:** [arXiv:2308.00352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00352)
  - **Focus:** Incorporates Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into prompt sequences and publish-subscribe message routing, reducing communication chaos and coordination overhead in complex workflows.

- **AgentVerse: Facilitating Multi-Agent Collaboration and Exploring Emergent Behaviors**
  - **Authors:** Weize Chen, Yusheng Su, Jingwei Zuo, Cheng Yang, Chenfei Yuan, Chen Qian, Hao Wang, et al. (Tsinghua University)
  - **Link:** [arXiv:2308.10848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10848)
  - **Focus:** Formalizes a structured execution lifecycle (Expert Recruitment $\rightarrow$ Collaborative Decision Making $\rightarrow$ Action Execution $\rightarrow$ Evaluation) with dynamic agent recruitment.

- **ChatDev: Communicative Agents for Software Development**
  - **Authors:** Chen Qian, Xin Cong, Wei Liu, Cheng Yang, Weize Chen, Yusheng Su, Yufan Dang, et al.
  - **Link:** [arXiv:2307.07924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07924)
  - **Focus:** Sequential multi-phase waterfall orchestration using peer chat chains (design, coding, testing, documenting) to solve software engineering tasks.

#### Advanced Dynamic Orchestration & Routing

- **Multi-Agent Collaboration via Evolving Orchestration**
  - **Authors:** Yufan Dang, Chen Qian, Xin Luo, Jinchao Fan, Zhong Xie, et al. (NeurIPS)
  - **Link:** [NeurIPS Proceedings / arXiv:2405.14838](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/multi-agent-orchestration)
  - **Focus:** Puppet-show orchestration framework featuring a centralized, adaptive orchestrator ("puppeteer") that dynamically schedules tasks, manages sub-agents, and evolves topologies during runtime.

- **DyLAN: Dynamic LLM-Powered Agent Network**
  - **Authors:** Fanrun Liu, Lingxiao Shen, et al.
  - **Link:** [arXiv:2310.02170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02170)
  - **Focus:** Dynamic DAG-based agent orchestration that scores agent contributions per reasoning step and prunes inefficient nodes to optimize inference cost and decision accuracy.

#### Comprehensive Surveys & Taxonomy

- **Multi-Agent Collaboration Mechanisms: A Survey of LLMs**
  - **Authors:** K. T. Tran, D. Dao, M. D. Nguyen, Q.-V. Pham, et al.
  - **Link:** [arXiv:2501.06322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06322)
  - **Focus:** Exhaustive survey detailing centralized, decentralized, layered, and competitive coordination mechanisms across current multi-agent systems.

- **A Survey on LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems: Workflow, Infrastructure, and Challenges**
  - **Authors:** X. Li, S. Wang, S. Zeng, Y. Wu, Y. Yang
  - **Link:** [Springer Open](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44336-024-00009-2)
  - **Focus:** Categorizes multi-agent communication topologies (fully connected, star, hierarchical, peer-to-peer), scheduling logic, memory synchronization, and consensus protocols.
