### Core Multi-Agent Orchestration Framework Papers

- **AutoGen:** [AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08155)  
  *Authors:* Qingyun Wu et al.  
  Introduces conversable, customizable agents integrating LLMs, human input, and tools to execute complex task workflows.
- **MetaGPT:** [MetaGPT: Meta Programming for A Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00352)  
  *Authors:* Sirui Hong et al.  
  Encodes Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into structured agent communication flows for multi-agent software engineering.
- **ChatDev:** [Communicative Agents for Software Development](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07924)  
  *Authors:* Chen Qian et al.  
  Introduces a chat-chain structure decomposing the software development lifecycle into sequential multi-agent dialogues.

### Comprehensive Survey and Review Papers

- **Foundational Survey:** [Large Language Model based Multi-Agents: A Survey of Progress and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01680)  
  *Authors:* Taicheng Guo et al.  
  Covers agent profiling, multi-agent communication schemes, environment simulation, and benchmarks.
- **Communication Architectures:** [Beyond Self-Talk: A Communication-Centric Survey of LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14321)  
  *Authors:* Bingyu Yan et al.  
  Examines system topologies, interaction protocols, and scalability bottlenecks.
- **Workflow Orchestration:** [A Survey on Agent Workflow -- Status and Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01186)  
  *Authors:* Chaojia Yu et al.  
  Classifies pipeline vs. graph orchestration flows, role assignment, and workflow specifications across 20+ systems.
- **Collaboration & Evolution:** [Beyond Individual Intelligence: Surveying Collaboration, Failure Attribution, and Self-Evolution in LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14892)  
  *Authors:* Shihao Qi et al.  
  Presents the LIFE framework addressing multi-round error propagation and collective self-refinement.
- **Debate Topologies:** [Multi-Agent Debate Strategies: Survey, Taxonomy, and Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26212)  
  *Authors:* Quim Motger et al.  
  Synthesizes 141 studies focusing on interaction structures, memory, and consensus protocols in multi-agent debate.
- **Communication Theory:** [The Five Ws of Multi-Agent Communication: Who Talks to Whom, When, What, and Why -- A Survey from MARL to Emergent Language and LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11583)  
  *Authors:* Jingdi Chen et al.  
  Maps agent coordination trade-offs across reinforcement learning and LLM paradigms.
