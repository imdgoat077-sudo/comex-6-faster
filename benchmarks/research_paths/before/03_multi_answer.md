Key research papers on multi-agent orchestration fall into foundational frameworks and comprehensive architectural surveys.

### Foundational Orchestration Frameworks

- **[AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08155)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08155))
  - **Authors:** Qingyun Wu, Gagan Bansal, Jieyu Zhang, et al.
  - **Focus:** Customizable, conversable agents integrating LLMs, humans, and tool orchestration.
- **[MetaGPT: Meta Programming for A Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00352)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.00352))
  - **Authors:** Sirui Hong, Mingchen Zhuge, Jiaqi Chen, et al.
  - **Focus:** Incorporates human Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into role-based multi-agent execution graphs.
- **[ChatDev: Communicative Agents for Software Development](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07924)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924))
  - **Authors:** Chen Qian, Wei Liu, Hongzhang Liu, et al.
  - **Focus:** Sequential multi-agent dialogue chains managing waterfall software development phases.
- **[CAMEL: Communicative Agents for "Mind" Exploration of Large Language Model Society](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17760)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.17760))
  - **Authors:** Guohao Li, Hasan Abed Al Kader Hammoud, Hani Itani, et al.
  - **Focus:** Inception prompting enabling autonomous cooperative multi-agent dialogue.
- **[AutoGen Studio: A No-Code Developer Tool for Building and Debugging Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15247)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.15247))
  - **Authors:** Victor Dibia, Jingya Chen, Gagan Bansal, et al.
  - **Focus:** Declarative orchestration and visual debugging interfaces.

### Survey and Architecture Papers

- **[Multi-Agent Collaboration Mechanisms: A Survey of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06322)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.06322))
  - **Authors:** KT Tran, D Dao, MD Nguyen, QV Pham, et al.
  - **Citations:** 820
  - **Focus:** Categorizes topologies into centralized, decentralized, layered, and shared-memory collaboration mechanisms.
- **[Multi-Agent Systems: A Survey](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/8352646/)** ([PDF](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/iel7/6287639/8274985/08352646.pdf))
  - **Authors:** A Dorri, SS Kanhere, R Jurdak
  - **Citations:** 1,811
  - **Focus:** Foundational distributed problem-solving, negotiation, and communication structures.
- **[Multi-Agent Coordination Across Diverse Applications: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14743)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.14743))
  - **Authors:** L Sun, Y Yang, Q Duan, et al.
  - **Citations:** 98
  - **Focus:** Task allocation and coordination structures across robotics, software, and LLMs.
- **[A Survey on Agent Workflow – Status and Future](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11082076/)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.01186))
  - **Authors:** C Yu, Z Cheng, H Cui, et al.
  - **Citations:** 58
  - **Focus:** Coordination graphs, runtime execution, and role assignments.
- **[The Orchestration of Multi-Agent Systems: Architectures, Protocols, and Enterprise Adoption](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13671)** ([PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.13671))
  - **Authors:** A Adimulam, R Gupta, S Kumar
  - **Citations:** 44
  - **Focus:** Enterprise workflow orchestration patterns and dynamic routing protocols.
- **[A Survey of Multi-AI Agent Collaboration: Theories, Technologies and Applications](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3745238.3745531)** ([PDF](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3745238.3745531))
  - **Authors:** X Zhang, X Dong, Y Wang, et al.
  - **Citations:** 42
  - **Focus:** Inter-agent communication protocols and consensus mechanisms.

### Findings Comparison

- **Agreement:** Sources complement each other without factual contradictions. One dataset identifies the primary open-source orchestration implementations (AutoGen, MetaGPT, CAMEL, ChatDev), while the other categorizes broader architectural taxonomies (centralized, decentralized, graph-based workflows).
- **Missing Information:** Citation counts were reported for survey papers but not retrieved for the foundational framework papers.
