### Surveys and Orchestration Taxonomies

  - **Authors:** Ahsan Bilal, Muhammad Ahmed Mohsin, Muhammad Umer, Muhammad Awais Khan Bangash, Muhammad Ali Jamshed

### Framework Architectures and Agent Collaboration

- [A survey on large language model based autonomous agents](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11704-024-40231-1)
  - **Authors:** Lei Wang, Chen Ma, Xueyang Feng, Zeyu Zhang, Hao Yang, Jingsen Zhang, Zhiyuan Chen, Jiakai Tang, Xu Chen, Yankai Lin, Wayne Xin Zhao, Zhewei Wei, Ji-Rong Wen
  - **Year / Venue:** 2024, Frontiers of Computer Science
  - **Focus:** Proposes a unified architectural model synthesizing agent construction, collaborative social coordination, multi-agent environment interaction, and framework abstractions including AutoGen, MetaGPT, and ChatDev.
- [The Rise and Potential of Large Language Model Based Agents: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.07864)
  - **Authors:** Zhiheng Xi, Wen-Xiang Chen, Xin Guo, Wei He, et al.
  - **Year / Venue:** 2023, arXiv preprint / Fudan NLP
  - **Focus:** Classifies agent society organization, role allocation, communicative topologies, and multi-agent collaborative schemes.
- [MultiAgentESC: A LLM-based Multi-Agent Collaboration Framework for Emotional Support Conversation](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.232)
  - **Authors:** Yangyang Xu, Jinpeng Hu, Zhuoer Zhao, Zhangling Duan, et al.
  - **Year / Venue:** 2025, Proceedings of EMNLP 2025
  - **Focus:** Implements role-specialized sub-agent orchestration for communicative dialogic strategy execution.
