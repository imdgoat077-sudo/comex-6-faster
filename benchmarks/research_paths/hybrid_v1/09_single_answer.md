Key research papers on Multi-Agent LLM Orchestration, categorized by foundational architecture, dynamic topology, scaling laws, and surveys:

---

#### 1. Foundational Orchestration Frameworks

* **AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation Framework**  
  * **Authors:** Qingyun Wu, Gagan Bansal, Jieyu Zhang, et al. (Microsoft Research)  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2308.08155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08155)  
  * **Focus:** Introduces customizable, conversable agents that orchestrate complex workflows through automated multi-turn dialogue, tool usage, and human-in-the-loop coordination.

* **MetaGPT: Meta Programming for A Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework**  
  * **Authors:** Sirui Hong, Mingchen Zhuge, Jonathan Chen, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2308.00352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00352)  
  * **Focus:** Encodes Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into agent prompt sequences and structured communication interfaces to reduce cascading hallucinations in software engineering pipelines.

* **CAMEL: Communicative Agents for "Mind" Exploration of Large Language Model Society**  
  * **Authors:** Guohao Li, Hasan Abed Al Kader Hammoud, Hani Itani, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2303.17760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17760)  
  * **Focus:** Proposes inception prompting to orchestrate cooperative role-playing between autonomous agents with minimal human intervention.

* **ChatDev: Communicative Agents for Software Development**  
  * **Authors:** Chen Qian, Xin Cong, Cheng Yang, et al. (Tsinghua University)  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2307.07924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07924)  
  * **Focus:** Implements waterfall-model multi-agent orchestration (designing, coding, testing, documenting) via structured multi-agent sub-chat chains.

---

#### 2. Dynamic Topologies and Adaptive Orchestration

* **Dynamic LLM-Agent Network (DyLAN): Dynamic LLM-Agent Network for Effective Multi-Agent Collaboration**  
  * **Authors:** Chen Liu, Ruoxi Sun, Xiang Shen, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2310.02170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02170)  
  * **Focus:** Replaces static message routing with dynamic communication architectures; dynamically selects top-contributing agents and routes queries through multi-layered consensus rounds.

* **AgentVerse: Facilitating Multi-Agent Collaboration and Exploring Emergent Behaviors**  
  * **Authors:** Weize Chen, Yusheng Su, Jingwei Zuo, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2308.10848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10848)  
  * **Focus:** Evaluates dynamic adjustment of agent groups, task decomposition, and execution topologies across centralized vs. decentralized orchestration modes.

* **SquidAgent: Parallelize Wisely, Coordinate Efficiently**  
  * **Authors:** Yexiong Lin, Shanshan Ye, Yu Yao, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2410.08647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08647)  
  * **Focus:** Addresses latency and token overhead in agent swarms by introducing adaptive parallelization vs. sequential hand-offs based on subtask dependency graphs.

---

#### 3. Scaling Laws and Theoretical Dynamics

* **More Agents Is All You Need**  
  * **Authors:** Junyou Li, Qin Zhang, Yanghua Xiao, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2402.05120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05120)  
  * **Focus:** Analyzes scaling properties of multi-agent collaboration, proving how sampling-and-voting orchestration ensembles scale performance orthogonally to model parameter count.

* **Know the Shape, Find the Fault: Topology-Conditioned Diagnosis of Multi-Agent LLM Failures**  
  * **Authors:** Xinwen Liu, Zhuocheng Pan, Isabella Zhu, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2410.10126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10126)  
  * **Focus:** Models interaction graph structures (stars, rings, complete graphs) to identify deadlock, error propagation, and failure modes across orchestration topologies.

---

#### 4. Comprehensive Surveys

* **Large Language Model based Multi-Agents: A Survey**  
  * **Authors:** Taicheng Guo, Xiuying Chen, Yaqi Wang, et al.  
  * **Link:** [arXiv:2402.01680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01680)  
  * **Focus:** Comprehensive taxonomy of multi-agent profiling, communication paradigms, cooperation/competition dynamics, and orchestration environments.
